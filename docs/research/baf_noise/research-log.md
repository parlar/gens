| 2026-09-06 | Oracle-bin comparison | Ran the Section 9 first comparison under a pre-registered protocol committed before the estimator (protocol-oracle.md, results-oracle.md). Controls exposed a defect in the locked threshold: the estimator is capped at f <= 0.5, so 54 percent of its balanced null sits on the boundary and the "3 SD below 0.5" rule had a measured 2.2 percent false-positive rate; it was replaced by a calibrated bootstrap percentile (measured 1.4 percent) before results were read. Locked outcome: INVALID. One noncarrier separated at chr22, a pericentromeric locus where only 3 of 50 heterozygous calls survive GQ >= 20 (median GQ 6 versus 44-48 elsewhere). The primary endpoint was also unreachable: no locus had as many separating samples as carriers. chr10 showed the expected pattern in two of three carriers (f-hat 0.336-0.369, p < 0.0001, zero noncarriers) but is insertion-derived with 75-94 percent of sites sharing fragments. The deletion arm's locked endpoint was not met because six of seven loci carry near-zero callable heterozygous sites in every sample; the one callable locus, chr1:189735377-189814226, gave a textbook positive with the carrier at 161 of 161 records homozygous against 96 heterozygous in a noncarrier. Conclusion: the duplication question cannot be answered with this cohort, the density channel responds correctly, and loci must be chosen against a pre-declared callability filter. No BAM was read, no data left the machine, H5 remains untested. |
